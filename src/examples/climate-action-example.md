# Climate Crisis: Time to Act

### Global temperatures rise 1.2°C above pre-industrial levels. We have less than 7 years to prevent catastrophic warming beyond 1.5°C.

## The Science is Clear

The evidence is overwhelming and undeniable:

- **CO2 levels** at highest point in 800,000 years
- **Arctic ice** declining 13% per decade
- **Sea levels** rising 3.3mm annually
- **Extreme weather** events increased 5x since 1970

## Impact on Communities

Climate change affects real people every day:

- 20 million climate refugees by 2025
- Food insecurity for 800 million people
- Economic losses exceeding $300 billion annually
- Health impacts from air pollution and heat stress

## Solutions Within Reach

We have the technology and knowledge to act:

- **Renewable Energy**: Solar and wind now cheaper than fossil fuels
- **Energy Efficiency**: Buildings can reduce emissions 50%
- **Sustainable Transport**: Electric vehicles and public transit
- **Nature-Based Solutions**: Reforestation and ecosystem restoration

## What You Can Do

Every action matters in the fight against climate change:

- Reduce personal carbon footprint
- Support climate-conscious businesses
- Advocate for policy changes
- Educate and inspire others

> The time for action is now. Together, we can create a sustainable future for all.
